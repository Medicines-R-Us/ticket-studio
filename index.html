HOW TO USE LABL

Make branded shelf tickets to print on plain A4 — either receive a ready-made batch and print it, or build labels yourself.

1 · Getting your labels — three ways

  • Share link — head office sends a link; click it and LABL opens already filled in. Print.
  • Load a batch / CSV — use Load batch, or paste/upload under Import (MIRA / Z Software).
  • A PDF — if you were sent a finished PDF, just print it (A4, 100%).

Or build your own: click Add label and type the details.

2 · Building & tweaking

  • Label type: Contemporary (clean outline) or Legacy (bold colour panel).
  • Prices: RRP, Our Price, Special — % OFF & SAVE are worked out for you. Green dot = % correct, red = check it.
  • Size 2/4/8/16 per A4 · Price size · Colour swatch per label · Copies · Flash tag · Multibuy (Qty + For).
  • Do lots at once: Select all, then Bulk change or Delete selected.

3 · Fold & cut

Fold on the dotted line at the top and hook the label over the shelf rail. Cut between labels on the dotted lines so every store matches.

4 · Printing

  • Click Hide panel to see the whole sheet, then Print.
  • Choose A4 and 100% (no scaling) — never "fit to page", or the labels shrink.

5 · Need a fresh list from MIRA?

With Claude + MIRA, just ask: "LABL all Panadol at Gregory Hills". You'll get a CSV, share link or PDF to use above. No access? Ask head office for the link or PDF.
